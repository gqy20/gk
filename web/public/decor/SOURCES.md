# High Exam Decor Assets

These assets are candidates for subtle "高考胜利" decoration in the app UI.

## Recommended

- `selected/noto-orange.svg`
  - Meaning: 一举高橙, 心想事橙.
  - Suggested use: small blessing badge in the header or province panel.
  - Source: Google Noto Emoji, `emoji_u1f34a.svg`.
  - License: Apache-2.0, see https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji.

- `selected/noto-apple.svg`
  - Meaning: 平平安安, 稳稳上岸.
  - Suggested use: small icon beside a blessing badge, paired with orange.
  - Source: Google Noto Emoji, `emoji_u1f34e.svg`.
  - License: Apache-2.0, see https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji.

- `selected/noto-scroll.svg`
  - Meaning: 金榜题名, 录取喜报.
  - Suggested use: result page or "future path" success state.
  - Source: Google Noto Emoji, `emoji_u1f4dc.svg`.
  - License: Apache-2.0, see https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji.

- `selected/certificate-uxwing.svg`
  - Meaning: 录取达成, 证书感, 胜利感.
  - Suggested use: line icon in compact UI, especially if colorful emoji feels too playful.
  - Source: UXWing certificate icon.
  - License note: UXWing states icons can be used for personal and commercial projects without attribution.
