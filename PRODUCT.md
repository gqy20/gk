# Product

## Register

product

## Users

High school students preparing college applications in China, plus parents who want a concrete sense of what a school or major could mean after enrollment. They use the product while comparing schools, majors, cities, risks, and future daily life.

## Product Purpose

The product turns college and major choices into understandable exploration: school data, maps, future previews, and a university-life simulator. Success means a student or parent can picture the next four years more clearly and make application decisions with less abstraction.

## Brand Personality

Calm, concrete, encouraging. The interface should feel trustworthy and focused, with copy that answers student and parent concerns in plain language.

## Anti-references

Avoid consultancy jargon, generic strategy language, decorative dashboards, and layouts that make students hunt for the next action. Future-preview copy should avoid vague terms such as industrial engineering, compound development, and path model when a direct description would work better.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next choice obvious.
2. Explain future life in student-facing language.
3. Keep data and simulation controls calm, readable, and task-first.
4. Use space to organize decisions, not to create empty distance.
5. Preserve trust by showing specific school, major, and campus context where available.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable Chinese typography, visible focus states, and sufficient contrast for form controls and guidance text. Motion should be limited to state feedback and respect reduced-motion preferences. Simulator content should avoid unnecessary gendered assumptions unless the user explicitly sets them.
